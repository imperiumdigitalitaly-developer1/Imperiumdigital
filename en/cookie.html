Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Cookie Policy describes the use of cookies and similar technologies on the Imperium Digital website.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website can store on the user's device while browsing.

Rules relating to cookies may also apply to other technologies that allow information on the user's device to be stored or accessed.

2. Cookies used by the site

The Imperium Digital site does not use profiling or advertising cookies, nor tracking systems aimed at behavioural advertising.

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to collect aggregate statistics on how the site is used: this is an analytics tool that relies on cookies, described in detail in point 3.

The technical services used for hosting and running the site may also use their own technical technologies necessary to provide the service. Such technologies are subject to the terms and notices of their respective providers.

3. Google Analytics 4

The site uses Google Analytics 4, a statistical analytics service provided by Google Ireland Limited, which uses cookies to collect aggregated information on the use of the site (pages visited, traffic source, duration of the visit).

The cookies set by Google Analytics 4 are mainly _ga and _ga_*, with a maximum duration of about 2 years.

By default, Google Analytics 4 truncates the user's IP address before storing it, reducing its direct identifying capability.

The data collected is processed by Google according to its own privacy policy. It is also possible to prevent data collection by Google Analytics by installing the opt-out add-on provided by Google.

4. Hosting — Vercel

The site is hosted via Vercel.

Vercel provides the infrastructure and content delivery services necessary for the site to work. Any technical technologies used by Vercel are functional to providing the service and are not used by Imperium Digital to carry out advertising profiling of users.

For information on data processing by Vercel, you can consult their privacy policy.

5. Google Fonts

The site uses the Bricolage Grotesque and IBM Plex Sans typefaces via Google Fonts.

Fonts are loaded through a connection to Google's servers.

This connection does not, in itself, constitute the use of a profiling cookie by Imperium Digital, but may involve transmitting to Google the technical data necessary to provide the requested fonts, including the IP address.

6. Links to external services

The site may contain links to external services, including WhatsApp, Instagram, Revolut and Stripe, as well as websites related to completed projects.

These services are not automatically loaded within the site's pages.

Any processing of data, including any use of cookies, by such services takes place after the user accesses the relevant service and is governed by the notices and terms of the respective operator.

7. Consent requirements

Technical cookies necessary for the site to function do not require the user's consent.

The Google Analytics 4 cookies described in point 3, while serving statistical rather than advertising purposes, are not among the strictly necessary cookies. Users can prevent their collection through the tools listed in point 3, or block them directly from their browser settings, as described in point 8.

8. Managing cookies from your browser

Users can control, block or delete cookies through their browser settings.

Disabling certain technical cookies may, in some cases, affect the correct functioning of certain features of the site.

9. Updates

This Cookie Policy may be changed if the services, technologies or tracking tools used by the site change.
