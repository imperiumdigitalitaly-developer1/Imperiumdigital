Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This Cookie Policy describes the use of cookies and similar technologies on the Imperium Digital website.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website can store on the user's device while browsing.

Rules relating to cookies may also apply to other technologies that allow information on the user's device to be stored or accessed.

2. Cookies used by the site

The Imperium Digital site does not use, for its own purposes, profiling or advertising cookies.

No analytics tools or tracking systems aimed at user profiling or behavioural advertising are used.

The site does not use its own cookies intended to track user activity for commercial purposes.

The technical services used for hosting and running the site may use their own technical technologies necessary to provide the service. Such technologies are subject to the terms and notices of their respective providers.

3. Netlify

The site is hosted via Netlify.

Netlify provides infrastructure services and, for the contact form, may handle submissions through Netlify Forms.

Any technical technologies used by Netlify are functional to providing the related services and are not used by Imperium Digital to carry out advertising profiling of users.

For information on data processing by Netlify, you can consult their privacy policy.

4. Google Fonts

The site uses the Bricolage Grotesque and IBM Plex Sans typefaces via Google Fonts.

Fonts are loaded through a connection to Google's servers.

This connection does not, in itself, constitute the use of a profiling cookie by Imperium Digital, but may involve transmitting to Google the technical data necessary to provide the requested fonts, including the IP address.

5. Links to external services

The site may contain links to external services, including WhatsApp, Instagram and websites related to completed projects.

These services are not automatically loaded within the site's pages.

Any processing of data by such services takes place after the user accesses the relevant service and is governed by the notices and terms of the respective operator.

6. Need for a cookie banner

The site does not currently use profiling or advertising cookies or tracking tools for which user consent would be required.

For this reason, no cookie consent banner is currently shown.

Should analytics, advertising, profiling or other consent-based tracking tools be introduced in the future, this Cookie Policy will be updated and the consent-gathering methods required by applicable law will be adopted.

7. Managing cookies from your browser

Users can control, block or delete cookies through their browser settings.

Disabling certain technical cookies may, in some cases, affect the correct functioning of certain features of the site.

8. Updates

This Cookie Policy may be changed if the services, technologies or tracking tools used by the site change.
