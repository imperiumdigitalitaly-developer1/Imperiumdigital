Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This notice describes the processing of personal data carried out in connection with the Imperium Digital website and the contact methods made available to users.

This notice is provided pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and applicable data protection law.

1. Who processes personal data

Imperium Digital is currently a digital project started by Giuseppe Fertitta and is not, at present, incorporated as a company.

For the processing activities described in this notice, Giuseppe Fertitta determines the purposes and means of the processing and therefore acts as data controller, pursuant to Article 24 of the GDPR.

For any request regarding the processing of personal data, you can contact:

Email: imperiumdigitalitaly@gmail.com

2. What personal data may be processed

Through the site and its contact channels, personal data voluntarily provided by the user may be processed.

In particular, the contact form may request:

  • full name;
  • email address;
  • business name, where provided;
  • business category, where provided;
  • type of project requested;
  • whether the user already has a website;
  • content of the message or request sent.

Personal data contained in communications that the user voluntarily chooses to send by email or through the contact channels listed on the site may also be processed, including any data contained in the text of the communication or in attachments.

The site may also contain external links to payment services via Revolut, through a Revtag, and Stripe, through a payment link. Data entered by the user on these platforms is processed directly by the respective providers according to their own notices and applicable terms. The Imperium Digital website does not directly collect complete payment card data through these links.

3. Purposes of processing

Personal data may be processed for the following purposes:

  • receiving and managing requests sent through the contact form;
  • responding to the user's requests;
  • understanding the needs related to the digital project described by the user;
  • evaluating and discussing a possible project or collaboration;
  • maintaining the communications necessary with the user regarding their request;
  • ensuring the technical security of the site and preventing or managing any misuse of the contact channels;
  • complying with any obligations required by law or by orders of the competent authorities;
  • managing any voluntary contributions intended to support the development of the project, limited to the information made available to the controller through the relevant payment service.

The data is not used for marketing, newsletter or profiling purposes based on the processing methods currently provided by the site.

4. Legal basis for processing

The processing of data provided through the contact form is carried out, to the extent necessary to respond to the user's request and to carry out pre-contractual steps at their request, on the basis of Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR.

For activities necessary for the security of the site, the technical management of communications and the protection of legitimate interests connected with the proper management of the project, the legal basis may be Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR, to the extent that such interests do not override the rights and freedoms of the data subject.

Where processing is necessary to comply with a legal obligation, the relevant legal basis is Article 6(1)(c) of the GDPR.

Any data processed in connection with a voluntary contribution is managed to the extent necessary to manage the transaction and any applicable obligations.

5. Provision of data

Providing the data requested in the contact form is necessary to the extent that such data is essential to understand and manage the user's request.

Failure to provide the necessary data may make it impossible to respond to the request or evaluate it adequately.

Any data marked as optional may be omitted by the user without, in itself, preventing the request from being sent.

Users are asked not to include in the form or in communications personal data of third parties that are not necessary for the request.

6. Processing methods

Personal data is processed using electronic tools and, where necessary, through communication tools used to respond to user requests.

Processing is carried out in compliance with the principles of lawfulness, fairness, transparency, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity and confidentiality set out in applicable law.

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are adopted in relation to the nature of the data and the activities carried out, with the aim of protecting it from unauthorised access, loss, destruction or alteration.

7. Managing the contact form

The contact form on the site is used to allow the user to send a request to Imperium Digital.

Based on how the site currently works, the request may involve opening the user's email program with a pre-filled message and, as a way of supporting the handling of the request, sending the data via Netlify Forms.

Netlify may therefore process the data transmitted through the service to the extent necessary to provide that functionality.

The data is not used for marketing or profiling purposes by Imperium Digital.

8. Communications via email and other channels

Users can contact Imperium Digital through the email address and other contact channels published on the site.

Data contained in the communication is processed in order to manage and respond to the request.

The amount and type of data processed depends on the content of the communication voluntarily sent by the user.

9. Voluntary contributions via Revolut and Stripe

The site may provide a number of links through which users can voluntarily choose to support the development of the Imperium Digital project.

The links currently used are:

  • Revolut, via a Revtag;
  • Stripe, via a payment link.

The buttons on the site are links to external services and do not constitute a payment system managed directly within the Imperium Digital site.

If the user decides to make a contribution, the processing of the data necessary for the transaction takes place on the respective provider's platform and according to their own terms and privacy notices.

Revolut states, among the data processed as part of its services, the Revtag and information relating to transactions.

The Stripe payment link may ask the user, in addition to card details, for their full name and email address, which Stripe needs to process the transaction and send the related receipt. This data is also processed directly by Stripe, according to its own privacy notice.

As with Revolut, the Stripe payment link also allows the user to choose the contribution amount freely.

Imperium Digital does not request payment service login credentials through its site and does not directly collect complete payment card data through the above links.

Any information relating to the contribution made available to the controllers by the respective provider may be processed to the extent necessary to manage the transaction and any obligations required by law.

The contribution is voluntary and is not required to browse the site or to send a contact request.

10. Recipients of data

Personal data may be processed by:

  • Giuseppe Fertitta, as data controller;
  • technical providers used for the operation of the site and related services, to the extent necessary to provide their respective functions;
  • providers of external services chosen directly by the user, such as Revolut, when the user accesses their platforms;
  • public bodies or competent authorities where required by law or by an order of the authority.

Personal data is not sold to third parties.

11. Third-party services

The site uses services provided by third parties for certain technical functions.

These include Netlify, used for hosting the site and, for the contact form, for the related Netlify Forms functionality.

When users access external services, the processing of their data by the relevant provider is governed by the documentation and notices published by that provider.

12. Transfers to third countries

Some service providers used for the operation of the site may process data outside the European Economic Area.

Where necessary, such processing is subject to the mechanisms set out in Chapter V of the GDPR.

In particular, when a third-party service is used directly by the user, as in the case of external payment services, the related data processing is also governed by the documentation and privacy notice of the respective provider.

13. Data retention

Personal data is retained for a period no longer than necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.

In particular, data relating to contact requests is retained for as long as necessary to manage the request, maintain relevant communications and, where applicable, manage pre-contractual or subsequent activities directly connected to the request.

Where retention is necessary to comply with legal obligations, to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim, or to manage any disputes, data may be retained for the period necessary for those purposes.

Once the necessary period has elapsed, data is deleted or, where technically and organisationally feasible, rendered no longer attributable to the data subject.

14. Cookies and similar technologies

Based on how the site currently works, Imperium Digital does not use its own profiling cookies or behavioural analysis or advertising tracking tools.

For detailed information on cookies and similar technologies, please refer to the Cookie Policy published on the site.

The links to Revolut on the site are external links. Any processing of cookies or similar technologies carried out by these platforms takes place on their respective external services and is not directly managed by Imperium Digital.

15. Google Fonts

The site may use fonts provided through Google Fonts.

When fonts are loaded directly from Google's servers, the user's browser establishes a connection with the provider's infrastructure. The processing of technical data associated with this connection is governed by the processing terms applicable to the service used.

16. Automated decision-making and profiling

Personal data collected through the site is not subject, by Imperium Digital, to automated decision-making processes that produce legal effects or significantly affect the user.

No user profiling is carried out for marketing purposes using data collected through the contact form.

17. Data subject rights

Where provided for by law, data subjects may exercise the rights recognised under Articles 15 et seq. of the GDPR, including the right to:

  • obtain confirmation as to whether or not personal data concerning them exists;
  • obtain access to their personal data;
  • obtain rectification of inaccurate data or the completion of incomplete data;
  • obtain erasure of data in the cases provided for by law;
  • obtain restriction of processing in the cases provided for by law;
  • object to processing in the cases provided for by applicable law;
  • obtain data portability where the right is applicable;
  • withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent.

Exercising these rights does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

To exercise your rights, you can write to: imperiumdigitalitaly@gmail.com

18. Right to lodge a complaint

Data subjects who believe that the processing of their personal data breaches applicable law have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority, as provided for by Article 77 of the GDPR.

For Italy, the supervisory authority is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (the Italian Data Protection Authority).

19. Updates to this notice

This notice may be updated when the way personal data is processed changes, when the services used by the site change, or when applicable law changes.

The updated version is published on this page with an indication of the relevant update date.
