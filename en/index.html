Visibility today

SEO and GEO: two different ways to be found.

People no longer search only on Google: more and more they ask an AI assistant directly. A well-built site needs to work well in both cases — that's why we factor them in from the design stage.

SEO

Getting found on search engines

Traditional search engine optimization: solid technical structure, fast loading times, content that matches what your customers actually search for, and a foundation Google can read and evaluate correctly. It's the foundation of online visibility.

GEO

Getting cited by artificial intelligence

More and more people ask ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI-generated answers directly which business to choose. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) focuses on clear, verifiable, well-structured content so these AI systems choose to cite you.

On every project we work on both fronts: a solid technical structure for classic search engines, and clear, verifiable content to be found through the new artificial intelligences too.
